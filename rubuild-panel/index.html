Features

Everything you reach for, beside the page you're on

🗂️ Start my day

Save the tabs you open every morning, such as Outlook, Teams and your work tools, and reopen them all with one click. Make as many sets as you like: “Morning”, “Client A”, “Month-end reports”.

💬 Fill-in templates

Save messages you send again and again. Write {{Name}} or any {{word}} in double brackets, and RuBuild Panel asks for it when you copy. {date}, {time} and {day} fill themselves.

🔎 Instant search

Type a word to search everything you've saved, and press Enter to copy the top result. Matching words are highlighted.

⏰ Reminders

Set a reminder in seconds with quick picks like “In 15 min” or “Tomorrow 9 AM”. It pops up on your computer when it's due, with Done and Snooze. Repeat every day, every weekday or every week.

🔒 Private by design

No account and no RuBuild servers: your items stay in your browser. It warns you before saving passwords or card numbers. Import links from Edge Collections or bookmarks, or right-click any page to save it.

📤 Share in one click

Share any link, message, ID or note by Email, WhatsApp or Microsoft Teams, or send it to your phone with a QR code made on your computer. Team packs (Pro) give your whole team the same approved templates, links and workspaces in one file.

Also included: pin your favourites, open the panel with Alt+Shift+Q, store the username you sign in with next to each link, and see how much time you've saved each week.

Questions

RuBuild Panel FAQ

Is RuBuild Panel free?

Yes. Free includes 50 items, 3 reminders and 1 workspace, with no time limit. Every new user also gets Pro free for 14 days.

What happens when the 14-day trial ends?

Nothing is deleted. You keep all your items. If you have more than the free limits, everything stays visible and usable, but you can't add more until you delete some or buy Pro.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-in and nothing to sign up for.

Which browsers does it work in?

Microsoft Edge and Google Chrome (version 116 or newer) on Windows, Mac and Linux.

Where is my data stored?

Only in your browser on your computer. RuBuild has no servers and never sees your data.

What happens if I close or restart my browser or computer?

Nothing is lost. Everything stays as it was.

What happens if I uninstall RuBuild Panel, reset my browser, or move to a new computer?

Uninstalling removes your saved items from that browser, and we can't recover them because we never have a copy. Before uninstalling or moving, click Save backup at the bottom of the panel. On the new computer, install RuBuild Panel and click Restore. Pro saves a backup automatically once a day to Downloads › RuBuild Panel Backups. Resetting browser settings usually just turns extensions off: turn RuBuild Panel back on in your browser's extensions page.

Will reminders pop up when my browser is closed?

Yes, if your browser is allowed to run in the background. In Edge: Settings › System and performance › "Continue running background extensions and apps when Microsoft Edge is closed". In Chrome: Settings › System › "Continue running background apps when Google Chrome is closed". Reminders missed while the computer was off appear as "Missed reminder" the next time the browser starts.

Can I save passwords in it?

No, and it warns you if you try. Passwords and card numbers belong in your browser's password manager.

Does it read the websites I visit?

No. It only reads a page's address and title when you choose to save that page, and your open tabs only when you click "Save my open tabs" (it asks your permission the first time).

Can I use my Pro key on more than one computer?

Yes. Your key is for your personal use on any of your own computers, in Edge or Chrome. Team members each need their own key.

I lost my licence key.

Email support@rubuild.com from the address you paid with, or with your Razorpay payment ID, and we'll send it again.

How do I pay?

Through Razorpay, a secure, RBI-regulated payment gateway. In India: UPI, card, net banking or wallet. Outside India: international card. RuBuild never sees your card or bank details.

Is it a subscription?

No. Pro is a one-time payment.

Will there be updates?

RuBuild Panel is complete and designed to keep working without updates. If a browser change ever needs one, it'll be free for everyone, including Pro owners.

Who makes it?

RuBuild Services, an independent software publisher in India. We also make RuBuild Puzzles.
