Privacy Policy

The short version: everything you save stays in your browser.

Last updated: 7 November 2026

RuBuild Panel ("the extension") is published by RuBuild Services ("RuBuild", "we", "us").

The short version: the extension doesn't collect, send or sell any personal data. Everything you save stays in your browser.

What the extension stores, and where

Your notes, links, saved text, IDs, reminders, workspaces (saved sets of tabs), settings, usage counts for the "time saved" line, and your Pro licence key if you buy one. All of it is saved only in your browser's extension storage on your computer. It is never sent to RuBuild or anyone else.

Files you create

When you click Save backup or Save team pack, or when Pro's daily automatic backup runs, a file is saved to your computer's Downloads folder (or wherever you choose). These files stay on your computer unless you choose to share them.

Permissions and why

  • storage: saves your items on your computer.
  • sidePanel: shows the panel beside your page.
  • alarms and notifications: your reminders and the daily backup.
  • contextMenus: the right-click "Save to RuBuild Panel" option.
  • activeTab: reads the current page's address and title only when you choose to save it.
  • downloads: saves backup and team pack files.
  • tabs (optional): asked only when you click "Save my open tabs", to list the tabs in that window.

What the extension does not do

No account, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no reading of page content, and no data sold or shared.

Sharing

When you use Share, the extension opens your email app, WhatsApp or Microsoft Teams with the text you chose, or shows a QR code made on your computer. What you send is between you and that service.

Purchases

Pro is bought on a page run by Razorpay Software Private Limited, our payment provider, under its own privacy policy (razorpay.com/privacy). We receive your name, email, amount paid and payment ID so we can send your licence key and support you. We never receive your full card, UPI or bank details. We keep purchase records as required by Indian tax and accounting law, and we don't use your email for marketing.

Your rights

You can delete everything the extension stores by uninstalling it. To ask about or delete your purchase record, email support@rubuild.com. We'll reply within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its date.

Contact: RuBuild Services · support@rubuild.com
