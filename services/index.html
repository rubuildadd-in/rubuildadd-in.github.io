Help with Microsoft's marketplace

From Partner Center sign-up to a certified, paid listing on AppSource.

What we help with

We've been through it ourselves, so we can guide you

We've set up Partner Center, verified our company as a publisher, configured tax and payouts, and submitted our own apps for Microsoft's review. We help small software companies and businesses do the same, remotely, by screen-share and email.

🪪

Partner Center setup

Enrolment in the Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Program, company verification, publisher verification, users and roles, MFA.

💵

Tax and payout setup

Payout profile, tax forms (such as W-8BEN for India-based publishers) and the documents Partner Center asks for. We help you complete the forms; this is not tax advice.

🛍️

Marketplace listing

Offer setup, listing text, keywords, screenshots and logos at the right sizes, and paid plans through Microsoft.

✅

Certification help

Preparing test notes and test accounts, and working through Microsoft's certification feedback. Microsoft makes the final decision; we can't guarantee approval.

☁️

Microsoft 365 and Teams basics

Users, licences, security defaults and MFA, Entra app registrations, and rolling out Teams apps to staff.

🎨

App listing makeover

Already listed but not getting installs? We refresh your listing text and visuals.

🎫

Microsoft support requests

We guide you to open the right Partner Center or Microsoft 365 support request from your own account, write it clearly, and follow it up.

🔐

Identity and access basics

Microsoft Entra sign-in, MFA, security defaults and app permissions, set up sensibly for a small business.
