About RuBuild

Focused Microsoft 365 apps, built honestly.

Who we are

RuBuild Services is a small software company. We build focused apps: RightOwner, an ownership directory for companies, in Microsoft Teams, RuBuild Panel, a work side panel for Microsoft Edge and Google Chrome, and RuBuild Puzzles, two calm puzzle games for Edge and Chrome.

Why we build this way

We build apps that live inside the tools people already use every day. Our Microsoft 365 apps use Microsoft sign-in and keep company data inside the company's own Microsoft 365. Our browser extensions keep everything in your own browser. Your data stays with you, not on our servers.

What we believe

  • Do one job well. Each app solves one clear problem.
  • No overpromising. We only describe what our apps actually do today.
  • Your data is yours. We design so we never need to see it.
  • Fair pricing. Free versions and clear prices. RightOwner's paid plans are one price per company, billed by Microsoft. RuBuild Panel Pro and RuBuild Puzzles Pro are one-time payments.
