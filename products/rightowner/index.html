Find

Find the right person in seconds

Type “payroll”, “VPN” or a colleague's name. RightOwner shows the owner, the backup and their job titles.

  • Search by item, category, description or person
  • One-click Teams chat or email to the owner

Stay current

Spot owners who have left, and hand over in one step

RightOwner checks Microsoft 365 and flags items whose owner left or whose account is disabled.

  • “Needs a new owner” filter
  • Move all of a leaver's items to someone new at once
  • Owner and backup roles both handed over
Trust

Ownership check-ups every 90 days

A directory nobody updates is worse than none. Check-ups keep it trustworthy.

  • Each item shows when the owner last confirmed it
  • Stale items are flagged automatically
  • “Ask to confirm” sends the owner a ready-written Teams message

Questions

RightOwner FAQ

Where is our data stored?

In a SharePoint list called “RightOwner Directory” on your company's SharePoint home site, inside your own Microsoft 365 tenant. RuBuild Services never receives it.

Why does it need admin approval?

Reading colleagues' profiles and writing to a shared SharePoint list need permissions that only a Microsoft 365 admin can approve for the whole organization. It's a one-time step.

Is it priced per user?

No. One flat price covers your whole company. Pro is for companies with up to 250 active people; Business is for any size.

What happens if we cancel?

RightOwner returns to the Free plan. Nothing is deleted, and everything you added stays visible.
