Winsprout for Microsoft Teams

Feel proud of your work. Remember all of it.

Winsprout is a free, private wins journal for Microsoft Teams and the web. Log what you are proud of in 30 seconds a day, grow from Seed to Forest, share proud moments with your manager or team, and turn a year of wins into a clear appraisal report.

Try it free on the webComing soon to Microsoft TeamsFor IT admins
  • Free
  • Microsoft Teams and the web
  • Saved in your own OneDrive or Google Drive
  • No ads
The problem

Why it exists

Appraisals run on memory

By review time, most of the year's good work is forgotten. Thirty seconds a day gives you a dated record of what you actually did.

Quiet work stays invisible

A short weekly summary helps people who don't talk about their work, or work remotely, get seen by their manager.

Daily

Log a win in 30 seconds

Write what you got done, pick a type and mark the big ones.

  • Six win types: delivered, fixed, helped someone, customer win, learned, idea or improvement
  • Small, Solid or Big
  • Forgot yesterday? Log it for any past day
  • See your week in a chart and copy a summary for your 1:1
  • Optional daily reminder in your Outlook calendar
Appraisal time

Your appraisal report, ready in a click

Choose a period: last 12 months, financial year April to March, calendar year or custom dates.

  • Totals at a glance, highlights, type of work, badges and a month-by-month table
  • A Word report saved to your own OneDrive or Google Drive
  • Or copy it into any appraisal form
Talents

Share your talents

Storyteller, singer, speaker, artist? Add your talents and share a talent moment whenever you like, without waiting for an HR event.

  • A few words, up to four photos and links to your video or audio
  • Posted from your own account to a Teams chat or channel you pick, after you confirm
  • Colleagues react and reply right in Teams
Growth

Grow, earn badges, share proud moments

Every win earns points and moves you up seven levels, from Seed to Forest. A streak that days off never break, a weekly goal and 18 badges, each with progress to the next one.

  • Share a win, a badge or your level with your manager's chat, your team or a channel
  • Badges and your level appear in your appraisal report
Privacy

Private by design

Winsprout is for you, not for monitoring.

Your data stays yours

Wins are saved in the app on your device and, if you connect it, in a private folder in your own OneDrive (Apps › Winsprout) or Google Drive (Winsprout). RuBuild Services has no server that receives your data.

You choose what to share

Your manager can't see your wins unless you share a summary or report. Talent posts go from your own account only when you press Post. Winsprout never reads your messages.

FAQ

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Winsprout is free, with no ads.

Can my manager see my wins?

No. Only you can, unless you share a summary or report yourself.

Where does it work?

In Microsoft Teams on Windows, Mac, the web, iPhone and Android, with a Microsoft 365 work or school account. Or in any modern browser at rubuild.com/winsprout, with a Microsoft account (work, school or personal), a Google account, or no account. Posting straight to Teams chats and channels needs a work or school account. The app is in English.

What happens to my wins if I change jobs?

A work OneDrive belongs to your employer. Download a backup or your Word report before you leave, or use Winsprout on the web with your personal Microsoft or Google account, so your record stays with you.

Does my company need to approve it?

Some companies need IT to approve new apps. Winsprout shows you who can approve it and gives you a ready-made request to send to your IT team.

Where do I get help?

See the Winsprout help page or email support@rubuild.com.
