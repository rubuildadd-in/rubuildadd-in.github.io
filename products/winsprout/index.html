Winsprout for Microsoft Teams

Remember everything you achieved this year.

Winsprout is a free, private wins journal inside Microsoft Teams. Log your wins in 30 seconds a day, keep a streak going, turn a year of small wins into a polished appraisal report, and share your talents with colleagues.

  • Free
  • Inside Microsoft Teams

Why it exists

Good work shouldn't be forgotten

🧠

Appraisals run on memory

By review time, most of the year's good work is forgotten. Thirty seconds a day gives you a dated record of what you actually did.

🔦

Quiet work stays invisible

A short weekly summary helps people who don't talk about their work, or work remotely, get seen by their manager.

Daily

Log a win in 30 seconds

Write what you got done, pick a type and mark the big ones.

  • Six win types: delivered, fixed, helped someone, customer win, learned, idea
  • Small, Solid or Big
  • Forgot yesterday? Log it for any past day
  • Optional daily reminder in your Outlook calendar
Appraisal time

Your appraisal report, ready in a click

Choose a period: last 12 months, financial year April to March, calendar year or custom dates.

  • Totals, highlights and a month-by-month list
  • A Word report saved to your OneDrive
  • Or copy it into any appraisal form
Talents

Share your talents

Storyteller, singer, speaker, artist? Add your talents and share a talent moment whenever you like, without waiting for an HR event.

  • A title and a few words, plus a photo or a link to your video or audio
  • Posted from your own account to a Teams chat or channel you pick, after you confirm
  • Or copy the post and paste it yourself
Motivation

Streaks and 17 badges

A weekly goal and a streak that days off never break. Pick your own working days.

  • Badges for streaks, big wins, helping others, learning and sharing talents
  • Copy a short weekly summary for your manager or 1:1
Privacy

Private by design

Winsprout is for you, not for monitoring.

🔒

Your data stays yours

Wins are saved in the app on your device and, if you connect it, in a private folder in your own OneDrive (Apps › Winsprout). RuBuild Services has no server that receives your data.

🙋

You choose what to share

Your manager can't see your wins unless you share a summary or report. Talent posts go from your own account only when you press Post. Winsprout never reads your messages.

IT admins

For IT admins

Winsprout uses only Microsoft Graph delegated permissions. It acts as the signed-in employee, only while they use it.

Questions

Winsprout FAQ

Is it free?

Yes, Winsprout is free.

Can my manager see my wins?

No. Only you can, unless you share a summary or report yourself.

Where does it work?

Microsoft Teams on Windows, Mac, the web, iPhone and Android, with a Microsoft 365 work or school account. The app is in English.

Does my company need to approve it?

Some companies need IT to approve new apps. Winsprout shows you who can approve it and gives you a ready-made request to send to your IT team.

Where do I get help?

See the Winsprout help page or email support@rubuild.com.
