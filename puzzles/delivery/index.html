Delivery Policy

How your Pro key reaches you.

Last updated: 6 November 2026

  • What you receive: a RuBuild Puzzles Pro licence key, a line of text starting with RBP1-. No physical goods are shipped.
  • How: by email to the address you entered at checkout.
  • When: usually within a few hours, always within 24 hours of successful payment.
  • How to use it: open RuBuild Puzzles, tap Get Pro, paste the key, tap Activate.
  • Not received? Check spam or promotions, then email support@rubuild.com with your Razorpay payment ID.
  • Shipping charges: none. Delivery is free and digital, worldwide.
