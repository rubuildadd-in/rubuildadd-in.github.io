Privacy Policy

The short version: nothing leaves your browser.

Last updated: 6 November 2026

RuBuild Puzzles ("the extension") is published by RuBuild Services ("RuBuild", "we", "us").

The short version: the extension doesn't collect, send or sell any personal data. Everything stays in your browser.

What the extension stores, and where

The extension saves your game progress (levels, daily streak, scores, settings such as sound and theme) and, if you buy Pro, your Pro key. This is saved only in your browser's own extension storage on your computer. It is never sent to RuBuild or anyone else.

What the extension does not do

  • No account, sign-in or personal profile
  • No analytics, tracking, cookies or advertising
  • No access to the websites you visit, your browsing history, tabs or files
  • No data sold or shared with anyone

Permissions

The extension asks only for the "storage" permission, to save your progress in your browser.

Copying and links

If you tap "Share my result" or "Share my score", the extension copies a short text to your clipboard so you can paste it yourself; nothing is posted anywhere. Buttons such as "Get Pro" and "Leave a rating" open pages on rubuild.com in a new tab.

Purchases

When you buy Pro, you pay on a page run by Razorpay Software Private Limited, our payment provider. Razorpay collects the payment details needed to process your payment, under its own privacy policy (razorpay.com/privacy). RuBuild receives your name, email address, amount paid and payment ID so we can send your Pro key and help you later. We never receive your full card, UPI or bank details. We keep purchase records only as long as needed for support and as required by Indian tax and accounting law, and we don't use your email for marketing.

Your rights

You can delete everything the extension stores by uninstalling it. To ask about, correct or delete your purchase record, email deepakdas@rubuild.com. We'll respond within 30 days.

Children

The extension doesn't collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the "Last updated" date.

Contact: RuBuild Services · deepakdas@rubuild.com
