RightOwner support

Setup steps, answers and how to reach us.

Need help? Email support@rubuild.com. We reply within 3 business days (Monday to Friday, India Standard Time).

Getting started with RightOwner

  1. Install RightOwner from the Microsoft Teams app store or Microsoft AppSource.
  2. A Microsoft 365 admin opens it first, approves the app's permissions, and clicks Create the directory. This creates a SharePoint list called “RightOwner Directory” on your company's SharePoint home site.
  3. Add items manually, or import a spreadsheet (CSV) with the columns: Item, Category, Description, Owner email, Backup email. Use More → Download CSV template for an example.
  4. Anyone with access to your SharePoint home site can search RightOwner in Teams. People with edit access to that site can add and update items.

Plans and billing

  • The Free plan includes up to 25 items. Pro and Business add unlimited items and include a 30-day free trial.
  • After buying on AppSource, sign in on the activation page and click Activate my plan.
  • To change or cancel, go to the Microsoft 365 admin center → Billing → Your products.

Common questions

Who can add or edit items?

Anyone with Edit access to your company's SharePoint home site. Everyone else can search and view. Your admin controls this in SharePoint.

What happens when an owner leaves the company?

RightOwner flags items whose owner's account is disabled or removed. Use Hand over their items to move them to someone new in one step.

How do I remove RightOwner and its data?

Uninstall the app from Teams, then delete the RightOwner SharePoint list. Your admin can remove the app's permissions in Microsoft Entra ID. If you had a paid plan, cancel it in the Microsoft 365 admin center.

Does RightOwner send our data anywhere?

Your directory data stays in your Microsoft 365. For paid plans, the app sends only your organization's Microsoft tenant ID to our billing service to check which plan you have.
